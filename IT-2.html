COURSE DETAILS

Course Overview

The "Modern IT Support Training: Part 2 | A+ (220-1101)" course is designed to provide participants with a foundational understanding of various computing devices, their components, and essential networking skills. By focusing on desktops, laptops, tablets, and smartphones, learners will gain insights into CPU, memory, power supplies, and peripheral devices. The curriculum also delves into networking basics, introducing network services and troubleshooting methodologies.

Course Structure

Students will explore core topics such as mobile devices, networking, hardware, and virtualization. Special emphasis is placed on practical skills that can be applied in real-world IT support scenarios, including troubleshooting techniques for hardware and networks. Although this version of the course does not include working labs, it remains valuable for its video-based content, providing substantial learning opportunities for those pursuing concept-based understanding.

LEARNING OUTCOMES

What You’ll Learn

  • Understand different computing devices and their components.
  • Gain knowledge of basic networking and network services.
  • Learn about mobile device hardware, displays, and connectivity.
  • Develop skills in troubleshooting hardware and networking issues.
