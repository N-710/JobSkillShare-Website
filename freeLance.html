What is the Skills-to-Income Program?

Transform Your Skills into a Sustainable Freelance Career

The Become a Freelancer | Skills-to-Income Program equips you with the knowledge and skills needed to turn your talents into a profitable freelance career.

Through a structured sequence of courses, you'll learn to navigate online freelancing platforms, create high-value service offerings, and build a sustainable business.

Key Learning Highlights
🔌
Freelance Fundamentals
Platform navigation, profiles & gig creation
🔒
Scaling Your Business
Pricing, sales & client retention systems
💻
Client & Project Tools
Fiverr, Upwork, Asana, Zoom & more
OUTCOMES

Skills You'll Build

Build 13 core skills across freelance operations and client-facing professional practice — the toolkit of a working freelancer.

Online Profile Setup

Set up and optimize online freelance profiles to attract clients.

Keyword Research & Project Tools

Research keywords and use project management tools to stay organized.

Contracts & Time Tracking

Manage contracts digitally and track time and invoicing accurately.

Build a personal brand and acquire clients through outreach.

Marketing & Client Communication

Market your services and communicate effectively with clients.

Sales Negotiation & Onboarding

Negotiate rates, handle objections, and onboard new clients smoothly.
