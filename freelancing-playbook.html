COURSE DETAILS

Course Overview

The Complete Freelance Playbook (Advanced Edition) offers a comprehensive guide to transforming your freelancing career.

This course provides a detailed, step-by-step system for navigating the complexities of freelancing while building sustainable growth, stronger client relationships and a professional business model.

Structured Freelance System

Learn how to establish a reliable operating system to streamline your freelance operations.

Master tools such as Asana, Trello, Slack, Monday.com, Google Drive, Calendly, Zapier, Notion and other productivity platforms to manage projects, communication, documentation and delivery more efficiently.

High-Value Service Creation

Discover how to create services that attract higher-paying clients.

Develop outcome-based offers, understand pricing psychology and learn how to position yourself as a specialist instead of competing primarily on price.

Mastering Sales & Closing

Build stronger skills for attracting quality prospects, conducting effective sales conversations and handling objections.

Learn techniques for closing deals confidently while establishing professional expectations and clear boundaries.

Client Retention & Long-Term Growth

Focus on delivering exceptional client experiences that can turn one-time projects into longer-term client relationships and retainers.

Learn how to upsell additional services without damaging client relationships and how to manage expectations throughout the project lifecycle.

Building Authority & Career Growth

Develop your personal brand and use professional platforms such as LinkedIn to improve your visibility.

Build a scalable freelance career roadmap designed to help you move from individual freelancing tasks toward a more structured, business-minded professional approach.

LEARNING OUTCOMES

What You’ll Learn

  • Establish an efficient freelance operating system.
  • Create and price high-value freelance services.
  • Conduct effective sales and negotiation processes.
