COURSE DETAILS

Course Overview

This course, "Networking Fundamentals for Cybersecurity," is designed to provide a foundational understanding pivotal for a career in cybersecurity. We focus on core networking essentials, serving as a stepping stone before advancing to complex concepts and specialized areas such as Cisco networking.

Course Content

Chapter 1: Networking Fundamentals Learn about the OSI and TCP-IP models, various network topologies, virtualization, IP addressing, and network services essential for understanding how networks operate.

Chapter 2: Network Implementations Explore networked devices, routing, bandwidth management, Ethernet switching, and wireless technologies, including 802.11 standards and cellular technology.

Chapter 3: Network Operations Focus on network operations, analyzing interface statistics, device logs, SNMP, and understanding environmental factors that impact networks.

Chapter 4: Network Security Delve into security concepts, cryptography, authentication methods, technology-based attacks, and best practices for network hardening and defense-in-depth strategies.

Practical Lab Opportunities

Engage with lab activities covering essential router and switch configurations and next-generation firewalls. Video demonstrations offer practical, experiential learning without overwhelming detail, setting the stage for further advanced courses.

Certification

Upon completion, our honor-based certification process validates your acquired skills and prepares you for more advanced learning opportunities.

LEARNING OUTCOMES

What You’ll Learn

  • Understand networking fundamentals and their application in cybersecurity.
  • Identify and implement basic network operations and security measures.
  • Gain familiarity with network virtualization and IP addressing schemes.
  • Develop foundational knowledge in device securing techniques.
