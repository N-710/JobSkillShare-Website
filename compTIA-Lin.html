COURSE DETAILS

Course Overview

This comprehensive course provides hands-on experience in managing, configuring, and maintaining Linux operating systems. Through a series of in-depth modules, you'll enhance practical skills essential for IT roles that require expertise in Linux environments. The course also aligns with the CompTIA Linux+ (XK0-005) certification exam, guiding you through the necessary objectives and competencies.

Course Structure

The curriculum begins with establishing a home Linux lab, which is essential for practical skill development. You'll dive into file and directory management, learning to navigate and manipulate the Linux filesystem effectively. Modules on access control, backup, and package management further build your operational expertise. Advanced sections cover identity management, remote connectivity, process management, and system service configuration.

You'll also explore storage management, LVM commands, and shared storage in Linux environments. To ensure security and efficiency, topics on scripting, container management, networking, and firewall configuration are provided. Finally, the course introduces authentication methods and version control with Git, culminating in real-world applications of Linux in IT projects.

Practical Labs

Each section is supported by hands-on lab exercises, enabling you to apply theoretical knowledge practically. These labs simulate real job tasks and are designed to prepare you for career advancement. Upon full completion of all modules, you will be eligible for a course completion certificate that showcases your ability to apply practical Linux skills in a professional environment.
