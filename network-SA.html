COURSE DETAILS

Course Overview

The Network & Systems Administration Core Skills course is designed for aspiring Network Engineers and System Administrators, providing the foundational knowledge necessary for these critical IT roles.

This beginner-friendly course equips you with practical skills to manage and troubleshoot networks and systems effectively.

Course Structure

The curriculum is structured to guide you through key topics such as Networking Basics, Network Security, Hardware Devices, Switching and Routing, Cloud Administration, and more.

You’ll start with theoretical lectures and then move on to demonstrations, followed by hands-on practice labs to cement your understanding.

Mindset for Success

Success in this course requires a commitment to learning the theoretical foundations before jumping into practical applications.

Understanding the "why" behind systems and network configurations is crucial to performing effectively in technology roles.

LEARNING OUTCOMES

What You’ll Learn

  • Understand core networking and systems administration concepts.
  • Perform common entry-level system administration tasks.
  • Troubleshoot networking issues with a structured approach.
  • Apply foundational knowledge to cybersecurity and cloud administration contexts.
